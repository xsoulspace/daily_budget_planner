# DailyBu Strategy Pillars (2026 → 2028)

Concise pillars for making the product exceptionally useful and profitable.

---

## 1. One number

- **Single question:** "How much can I spend today?"
- Every feature either **gets** that number (amount, expenses, next salary) or **uses** it (daily/weekly, prediction).
- No feature that doesn't serve this. Focus is a feature.

---

## 2. Useful = fast and true

- **&lt;2 seconds** from open to number. No mandatory onboarding blocking the answer.
- **"Amount left"** is the weak link: reduce manual entry. Bet on **read-only bank/aggregator link** (2026–2027) so the number feels true.
- One number, two timeframes (daily + weekly). Perfect these before adding more.

---

## 3. Premium = obvious

- Current sell (sync + more tags) is weak. Reposition:
  - **"Your budget, on all devices, always in sync."**
  - **"One number for you and your partner."** (Household / shared budget = paid tier.)
- One clear subscription tier (e.g. monthly + yearly). No ad clutter in the core flow.

---

## 4. Free stays generous

- Core value (the one number, daily and weekly) stays **free**.
- Premium = sync + household + peace of mind. Grow adoption; convert on "everywhere" and "together."

---

## 5. Roadmap

| 2026 | 2027 | 2028 |
|------|------|------|
| One screen, one number, &lt;2 sec | Widget + Watch | Open banking / auto "amount left" |
| Premium = sync + household, one plan | Household as hero | AI suggestions (still one number) |
| No "more tags" as main sell | — | B2B / white-label option |

---

## One line

**"How much can I spend today? Right, instant, everywhere. Pay when you want it everywhere and with someone else."**
